ACTIVITY 8.4

Let us listen to a qawwali. A qawwali is a form of devotional music that is related to the Sufi Islamic tradition. Traditionally, qawwalis have been performed by a group of male musicians, however today, many female musicians perform qawwali, showing how musical forms can evolve and adapt over time.

Some famous female qawwali singers include Roshanara Begum, who was born in Kolkata.
