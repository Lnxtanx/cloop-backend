Birju Maharaj

Awadh. His father was the famous Kalka Prasad and uncle Binda Deen, who consolidated the Lucknow Gharana of Kathak. Trained under his uncle Binda Deen. Later, he established a post-independence with his elder brother Acchan Maharaj repetorrie of dance. He made his debut in Lucknow in 1926 at the All India Music Conference after which he performed regularly as a soloist, and with his elder brothers Acchan and Lacchu Maharaj. He moved to Delhi in 1955 and taught Kathak at the Shriram Bharatiya Kala Kendra, establishing the tradition of the Lucknow Gharana in Delhi. He was very famous for his abhinaya and bhav batana, and he performed such in the court style of sitting and enacting. He taught his nephew, the famous Birju Maharaj and many other famous Kathak dancers of India. He is survived by his sons Ram Mohan and Kishan Mohan who are also accomplished dancers, and gurus. He was the recipient of the Padma Shri in 1958 and Sangeet Natak Academy Award in 1967.

Kedar Nath Sahu was one of the leading exponents of Seraikella Chhau. He comes from a traditional family of Chhau dancers belonging to the Kanasari caste, who are engaged in these dance practices along with their main occupation of making household utensils. He started learning the dance form at a very early age from his father and grandfather. The dance form followed a Guru-Sishya Parampara and the art was orally transmitted from generation to generation. He performed in the troupe of Kumar Bijoy Pratap Singh Deo but later formed his own troupe with his disciples. Recipient of the Sangeet Natak Academy Award in 1981 and the Padma Shri in 2005. His performances received acclaim both in India and around the world. Many illustrious dancers trained under him as his students; the notable ones being Shashadhar Acharya, Gopal Prasad Dubey and Sharon Lowen to name a few.
