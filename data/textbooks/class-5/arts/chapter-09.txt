Here are some pointers on how to review and share feedback —

Make notes: Write what you noticed in a few words. You can elaborate on that after the show. If you do not write it down immediately, you may forget or miss it later. So, always remember to carry a note pad and a pencil.

Watch carefully: Be fully present during the show. Try not to be distracted. Observe all details on-stage. From small props to costumes, music and acting.
