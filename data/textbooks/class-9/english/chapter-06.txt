II Read the highlighted word in the line given below.

To make sure I pull through

The meaning of the verb ‘pull’ is ‘move or remove

something’. The preposition ‘through’ means ‘from one end or side to the other’. However, when they come together they convey a completely different meaning. Here, ‘pull through’ means ‘to succeed in doing something very difficult’.
