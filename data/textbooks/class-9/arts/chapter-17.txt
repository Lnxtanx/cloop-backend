Printmaking

Techniques in printmaking allow artists to make multiples of the same artwork in editions. The processes involve creating an image on a surface like a woodblock, metal plate, stone, linoleum, etc., with carving/ engraving tools and mediums that make it suitable for inking. After the surface is inked with a roller, a sheet of paper is placed over it and pressed either manually or using a printing press. In the case of wood-block printing on textiles, the inked wood block is pressed onto the fabric in specific patterns.

Photography

It involves creating artwork using digital tools, software applications, still/ video cameras. It may be clarified that photography is different from photo journalism.

Fig. 17.5. A camera lens

Photo journalism focuses mainly on documenting events, people, places, and nature for reportage.

Fine art photography might include documentation but also extends to explorations in image-making, visual effects, compilation, and photo-montage (compilation of different photographs/ images).

Heritage crafts

Fig. 17.7. Wood-block printing

These include a rich variety of arts traditions that involve hand- crafting and simple machines to design and produce objects, fabrics, toys, and artefacts for various everyday needs of people. India is a treasure trove of heritage crafts that specialise in various materials and processes.

Many of these traditions have been integrated with other design disciplines like product design, furniture design, ceramics, glass, textiles, and so on.

Arts research and management

Let us explore the importance of Art History and its connection to museology:

Art History is a discipline where one studies the development of art in various geographic regions across time and the social, cultural, and environmental factors that influenced changes.

Madhurima

Museology is another discipline which gives evidences of Art History. It is an important discipline that involves museum management, museum education, and preservation (restoration and conservation).

Disciplines that are closely related to the Visual Arts

Architecture combines structural engineering and design to make buildings for a variety of functions.

Archaeology combines various branches of science and history to study artefacts and other evidences that help us formulate a picture of the past.
